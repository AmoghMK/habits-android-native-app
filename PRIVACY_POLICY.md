# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026

## Overview
Amogh MK ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the **Habits** Android application handles your data. 

## Data Collection and Storage
**We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.** 

The Habits app is designed with an "offline-first" architecture. All data you enter into the app—including your habits, timers, and completion history—is stored **locally on your device** using a local SQLite (Room) database. 

## Internet Access
The app does not require internet access to function and does not connect to any external servers, analytics providers, or third-party tracking services.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the app does not collect user contact information, we encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at the email provided on our Google Play Store listing.
